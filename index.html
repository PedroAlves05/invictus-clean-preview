Cleaning Services Adelaide FAQs

What time do you arrive on site?

We have a completely flexible approach and will attend your premises at the time that suits your use of the site. This can be early mornings, before any of your staff arrive, during the day, or in the evening after everyone has left for the day.

How often do you clean a site?

Our service is tailored entirely to your requirements. For some, we will only attend once or twice per week. For other larger clients, we will have many cleaners on site seven days per week or even multiple times during the day. We build our service around what you need.

Do you supply everything required?

Yes. Generally, we supply all the labour, equipment, machinery and materials required to carry out all our cleaning tasks within the agreed price. Additionally, we regularly supply consumables such as toilet rolls, hand towels, bin liners etc., which would be invoiced at the end of each calendar month.

What are your hourly rates?

We believe our prices are very competitive, but do not publish a fixed hourly rate. All jobs are priced according to the service required, whether it be a domestic or commercial service, a builders clean or a one-off deep clean. A number of variables will impact the final calculation of the rate such as equipment and materials.

Are you fully insured?

Each cleaner, as a subcontractor, have their own business insurance cover which, amongst other things, covers Public Liability for at least AUS 5,000,000.

Can you unlock/lock and alarm the building?

Yes. This is a regular service offered to all our clients, large or small. This enables us to carry out our cleaning service outside normal working hours, thus minimising disruption to your staff.

Are we issued a cleaning contract?

Yes. Contracts are signed before going ahead with any work.

How do we pay you?

Both Commercial and Residential clients are invoiced at the end of each calendar month and are expected to proceed with the payment on the fortnight from invoice date.
