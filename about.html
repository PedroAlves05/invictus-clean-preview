About Invictus Clean Adelaide

Our vision, mission and values are the source of inspiration for continuously improving our future and growth.

Our Vision:

To become South Australia’s most trusted cleaning services provider.

Our Mission:

To help our clients maintain and improve the quality of their working/living environment by providing high standard and cost-efficient cleaning solutions.

Our Values:

  • TRUST

    The foundation for a safe and healthy collaboration. We strive to facilitate open, honest and caring communication, giving everyone the total confidence to work with us.

  • PEOPLE FIRST

    We are not a system working for a system. We are people collaborating with people. Therefore respect, kindness and positive feedback are essential to us.

  • QUALITY

    We pride ourselves on delivering the highest quality cleaning services, with integrity and reliability. We are also constantly looking for innovative methods and equipment for better outcomes and less environmental impact.

  • COST-EFFICIENCY

    We don’t promise the cheapest rates. However, we strive to find the most cost-efficient, tailored cleaning solution for businesses and homes.

A New option in the local cleaning industry

Invictus Clean (formerly Bravo Clean) is a family-run company, operating in Adelaide since 2021.

Born from our experience in South Australia cleaning industry, we have slowly built an excellent reputation for our energy, attention to detail and reliability. Steadily, new businesses, home owners and real estate agencies have been requesting our services, forcing us to expand and build a team.

By the end of 2023 Invictus Clean Pty Ltd has been registered as a private company with this simple mission to help our clients maintain and improve the quality of their working/living environment by providing high standard and cost-efficient cleaning solutions.

Frequently asked questions

What does “Invictus” mean?

Invictus means unconquerable or undefeated in Latin. It is also the name of a poem written by William Ernest Henley in 1875, which has been a source of inspiration for our company. Through all adversities and misfortunes life can put us through, we want to be that undefeated partner everyone can rely on.

Do you have high staff turnover?

We have a small but very committed team. We aim to be the employer of choice in the cleaning industry, as such we provide our staff with appropriate training and competitive remuneration.

Will we always have the same cleaner?

Yes, as far as is practical. We always try to maintain continuity by using the same staff on a client’s contract. This helps to build extensive knowledge of the site, a healthy relationship and trust between the cleaning team and the client, and leads to increased security for our customers.

Do your staff receive training?

Yes. Each of our cleaners receives comprehensive in-house and in-situ training in all areas required for them to skilfully perform their duties. All Health and Safety issues and key company policies are covered in their initial training and induction.

Are all your staff vetted?

Yes. As part of our recruitment process, following interview, we request a previous employer’s reference and required proof of address and entitlement to work in Australia. We also require a copy of their passport and driving licence for our records. We also require current police check and business insurance certificates to ensure additional security for our clients.

Will the changeover to Invictus Clean be smooth?

Of course. We have experience in dealing with new office cleaning contracts, which allows us to be thorough with planning and client liaison in order to make the changeover a seamless process. The only difference you will notice is the planned improvement in service and standards.

Can you provide references?

Yes. Once you have received the formal quotation from us and are serious about going ahead, we will be happy to provide you with customer references to help you finalise your decision.

If you have any other questions, don’t hesitate to contact us.
